Past markets

Every stretch of market history since 1928.

A steady return is a useful guess, but markets don’t move steadily. So Everglen replays your plan as if you’d retired in each year since 1928 — living through the real stock, bond and bill returns, and the real inflation, that followed — and counts how often your money lasted.

Sturdy Shaky Fragile

A sturdy plan lasted through almost every market, including most of the worst. A fragile one ran out more often than it lasted.

Private by design

No account. Nothing linked to a bank.

You type in your savings, and your plan lives on your phone. We never see your name, your email, your savings or any amount from your plan.

Kept on your phone
Your plan is stored in the app, with a copy in your own iCloud or Google backup so it comes back on a new phone.
No sign-up, no bank links
There is nothing to log in to, and Everglen never connects to banks or brokerages.
No ads, no tracking
No advertising, no tracking across apps, and nothing sold. Read the privacy policy.
