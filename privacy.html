Privacy policy

Everglen is a retirement planner for iPhone and Android, made by Wally Apps LLC, a California company. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is kept, and the few things that leave your phone.

Last updated: September 2026

No account, and nothing linked to a bank

Everglen has no sign-up and no login, and it never connects to banks or brokerages. Your plan stays on your phone and in your own iCloud or Google backup. We (Wally Apps) never receive your name, your email, your savings or any amount from your plan — only anonymous analytics about how the app is used.

What you enter

To build your plan, you type in details such as your age and your partner’s, your savings (including account balances and any names you give your accounts), what you spend, your income, Social Security, planned purchases, taxes, and updates to your savings over time. You decide what to enter; nothing is read from your bank or any other app.

Where it’s kept

  • On your phone, in the app’s own storage.
  • On iPhone, a copy is kept in your own iCloud (iCloud key-value storage, tied to your Apple ID), so your plan comes back on a new or replacement iPhone. It sits in your Apple account; Wally Apps can’t see or access it.
  • On Android, your plan is included in your phone’s Google backup (Android Auto Backup) if backup is turned on, so it comes back when you restore a new phone. It sits in your Google account; Wally Apps can’t see or access it.

Sharing your plan as a PDF

Everglen Plus can make a PDF of your plan. It’s made on your phone and goes only where you send it from the share sheet — for example mail, messages, Files or a printer. Nothing is uploaded.

Purchases

Everglen Plus is paid for through Apple (the App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details.

We use RevenueCat to check purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the purchases you’ve made and their receipts, your device and operating system, and your country and IP address. It does not receive your name, your email or anything from your plan. The app contacts RevenueCat only when the Plus offer is shown (once after you finish setting up, or when you tap a Plus feature), when you buy or restore a purchase, and when the app opens if you have Plus. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics

Everglen uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used. It records anonymous events about the screens you open and the features you use — for example that setup was completed, whether the plan is for a couple, how many accounts it has, a chosen Social Security claiming age, that the Plus offer was viewed, or that a purchase was started. Firebase also records an anonymous app-instance ID, your device model, operating system and language, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Analytics never includes your name, your balances, your spending, the names you give your accounts, or any other amount from your plan. It doesn’t use your device’s advertising ID, and it isn’t used for advertising.

In the European Union and European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics collection is switched off, and no consent prompt is shown. Firebase’s data practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Nothing else

Apart from RevenueCat and Firebase, above, and your own iCloud or Google backup, nothing leaves your phone. Everglen shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers — it doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. It has no crash-reporting service. We don’t use data brokers, and we don’t sell any data.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Your details → Clear my details and start over. This deletes your plan, its history and your saved questions — on your phone and in the iCloud copy.

Deleting the app removes what’s stored on your phone. On iPhone, the iCloud copy stays in your Apple account until you reinstall Everglen and start over. On Android, what’s in your Google backup is managed in your Google account.

Purchases are kept by Apple or Google, not by us, and can be restored on a new phone.

Analytics events are anonymous — not tied to your name, email or plan — so they can’t be looked up or deleted for one person; Google deletes them automatically after a set retention period.

Children

Everglen is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Not financial advice

Everglen gives estimates based on the details you enter. It is not financial, tax or legal advice.
